# sort-bookmarks

A command-line tool that lets you explore, search, and tidy up your browser bookmarks

Works with **Chrome, Brave, Edge, and Chromium** on macOS, Linux and Windows (Powershell)

## What it does

- **List all your URLs** pull out every unique bookmark link, deduped and sorted
- **Browse by folder** see your bookmarks organized the way your browser organizes them
- **Sort by date** find out what you bookmarked first, or what's newest
- **Search by title or URL** filter down to just what you're looking for
- **Find duplicates** spot URLs you've bookmarked more than once, grouped together
- **Get a quick summary** see totals, your oldest and newest bookmark, and your most-used folders
- **Check for broken links** test which URLs are still alive and which have gone dark
- **Filter by date range** only show bookmarks added before or after a certain date
- **Work with multiple browsers** switch between Chrome, Brave, Edge, and Chromium with one flag
- **Support multiple profiles** target a specific browser profile if you have more than one
- **Export to spreadsheet** every command supports tab-separated output you can paste straight into Excel or Google Sheets
- **Color-coded output** easy-to-read results with color highlighting in your terminal
